About Ruth

A Voice, A Worshipper, A Heart For Jesus.

Everything Ruth Asong does is centred around one purpose: making Jesus known. Through worship, music and ministry, her desire is to create moments where people encounter the presence of God.

Her journey is one of faith, obedience and a deep commitment to serving God through the gift of music.
